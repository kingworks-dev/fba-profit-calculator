# Free Amazon FBA Profit Calculator

Work out net profit per unit, margin, ROI and break-even price for an Amazon FBA product in seconds. No signup, no tracking, runs in your browser.

**Use it free:** https://kingworks-dev.github.io/fba-profit-calculator/

## What it calculates

- Net profit per unit after Amazon referral fee, FBA fulfillment fee, inbound shipping and storage
- Profit margin and ROI on product cost
- Break-even sale price
- Monthly net profit from units sold per month
